# Privacy Policy — Unscramble

_Last updated: [DATE]. Template — have it reviewed for your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA, COPPA) before publishing at EXPO_PUBLIC_PRIVACY_URL._

**Who we are.** Unscramble is operated by [COMPANY NAME, ADDRESS]. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

**Data we collect**
- *Guests:* progress is stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to us unless you opt in to anonymous analytics.
- *Account holders:* email address, username, avatar choice, game results (words played, scores, timings, hints) and synced progress (achievements, unlocked items).
- *Anonymous analytics (opt-in):* gameplay events such as "game completed", score and difficulty, linked to a random install identifier and, if signed in, your user id. No advertising identifiers, contacts, location or precise device identifiers.

**How we use data.** To provide the game, sync progress, compute leaderboards (username, avatar, score and level are visible to other players), prevent cheating, provide support and improve difficulty balance. Legal bases (GDPR): contract (account features), legitimate interests (anti-cheat, security), consent (analytics).

**Sharing.** We use Supabase (database, authentication, hosting) as a processor. If you buy Premium, purchases are processed by Apple/Google (and RevenueCat on our behalf); we receive only entitlement status. We do not sell personal data.

**Retention.** Account data is kept until you delete your account. Analytics events are deleted after 13 months.

**Your rights.** Delete your account and all associated data at any time in Settings → Data (immediate). Withdraw analytics consent in Settings. Contact us to access or correct your data.

**Children.** Suitable for all ages; children can play in guest mode without providing personal data. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent.

**Security.** Data is encrypted in transit and at rest and protected by row-level access controls.

**Changes.** We will post updates here and update the date above.
